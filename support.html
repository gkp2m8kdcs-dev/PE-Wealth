Before you email — quick answers

Where is my data stored?

All PE Wealth records, photos, and preferences are stored locally on your device, in the App's private sandbox. The App does not upload your data to a server. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I move PE Wealth to a new device?

PE Wealth data lives inside the App's sandbox. The most reliable way to move to a new device is to export your data from the App's settings (Export), transfer the file to the new device (for example via iCloud Drive, AirDrop, or email), then import it after installing PE Wealth on the new device.

How do I reset the App?

Open PE Wealth's Settings (or Preferences) inside the App and choose Reset. This permanently removes all locally stored holdings, transactions, goals, photos, and snapshots from the App's sandbox. Export first if you want to keep a copy.

Why does PE Wealth ask for camera or photo access?

Only to let you attach a photo to a transaction — for example, a receipt or a statement. Photos you attach are stored inside the App. We do not upload them. You can revoke camera or photo access at any time in iOS Settings.

Does PE Wealth provide live prices or connect to my brokerage?

No. PE Wealth is intentionally a journal and an analyzer, not a market data terminal. Prices are entered by you, and the App does not connect to brokerages, exchanges, or wallets.

Does PE Wealth provide investment advice?

No. PE Wealth is a personal journaling and analysis tool. Nothing in the App constitutes financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. The App includes an in-app disclaimer that asks you to acknowledge this on first use.

Is there a subscription or in-app purchase?

No. PE Wealth has no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no ads.

How to write a great support email

To help us help you faster, please include:

  • Your iOS version (Settings → General → About → Software Version).
  • Your device model (for example, iPhone 14 Pro).
  • The PE Wealth version number (shown on the App's Settings screen).
  • A clear description of what you were doing and what happened.
  • Screenshots, if you can share them.
