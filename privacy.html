1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the PE Wealth mobile application ("PE Wealth", "the App", "we", "us", "our") available on the Apple App Store. It explains what information the App handles, how that information is handled, and the choices you have.

PE Wealth is designed as a private personal wealth journal. The App does not connect to a brokerage, exchange, wallet, market data provider, or any other financial network.

2. What we collect

Because the App is offline-first, the only data the App handles is the data you enter.

  • Financial records you create — holdings, transactions, cash entries, goals, net worth snapshots, and free-text notes.
  • Photos you attach — images of receipts, statements, or other supporting documents that you choose to attach to a record.
  • App preferences — your base currency, the state of the disclaimer acknowledgment, and similar local settings.

We do not collect identifiers such as your Apple ID, advertising ID, device ID, location, or contact list. We do not collect usage analytics, crash reports through third-party SDKs, or any behavioral data.

3. How we store your data

All financial records, photos, and preferences you create in PE Wealth are stored locally on your device, in the App's private sandbox and (if you choose to enable it) in your device's local backup. The App does not upload this data to a server that we operate.

If you delete the App, the data stored in its private sandbox is removed with it. Standard device backups made by you or your device may continue to contain a copy until those backups are also deleted or overwritten; that is controlled by you, not by us.

4. Permissions we request

The App only requests the following operating-system permissions, and only when you actively use the feature that needs them:

  • Camera — to capture a photo of a receipt or statement to attach to a transaction. The photo is saved into the App's local storage. We do not transmit it anywhere.
  • Photo Library (read) — to let you attach an existing photo from your library to a transaction.
  • Photo Library (write) — only used if you choose to export a portfolio snapshot or report image to your library.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time in iOS Settings. Revoking a permission will not delete data already saved inside the App.

5. Third parties

PE Wealth does not embed third-party analytics, advertising, social, or tracking SDKs. The App does not use third-party crash reporting services.

The App is distributed through the Apple App Store. Apple may, as the platform provider, collect information governed by Apple's own privacy policies. We do not control, and are not responsible for, data handled by Apple.

6. Children's privacy

PE Wealth is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or such higher age as your jurisdiction applies). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App contains no features intended for children.

7. Your rights and choices

Because your data stays on your device, the rights you have are exercised directly inside the App:

  • Access — every record is visible inside the App at any time.
  • Edit or delete — you can edit or delete any holding, transaction, note, goal, photo, or cash entry.
  • Export — you can export your records to a portable file format at any time.
  • Reset — you can erase all App data from inside the App's settings.

If you wish to exercise a right that requires our involvement — for example, because you believe we hold information about you — contact us at the address below. Given the App's offline design, the most common answer is that we do not hold that information.

8. Export and reset

The built-in export feature produces a self-contained file containing the records you selected. That file is saved by you, in a location you choose (for example, the iOS Files app, or your photo library for image exports). Once a file leaves the App, it is governed by whatever protections apply to that storage location.

The built-in reset feature removes all locally stored records, photos, and preferences from the App's sandbox. It does not delete files you have already exported.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If a change is material, we will make it visible inside the App or on this page before it takes effect.
