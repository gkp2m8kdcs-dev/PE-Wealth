How PE Wealth works.

A quiet workflow built around clarity, not alerts.

1. Capture

Add positions and transactions as they happen. Attach a photo of a receipt or statement. Write a note explaining the why.

2. See

Watch net worth, allocation, and cashflow update in real time. Compare months, quarters, and years on the same canvas.

Net worth Cost basis Cashflow

3. Reflect

Review concentration risk, diversification, and goal progress. The journal framing turns raw numbers into decisions you can revisit.

4. Own

Export everything as a portable archive. Reset the app to a clean slate whenever you want. Nothing is locked in.

Privacy, by construction.

PE Wealth is offline-first. The data you record never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

No account

No signup, no email, no login. Open the app and start writing.

No cloud sync

There is no remote server holding your holdings, transactions, or notes.

No live market feeds

Prices are entered by you. Nothing connects to brokerages, exchanges, or wallets.

No tracking

No analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers, no notifications, no in-app purchases.

Photos stay local

Camera and photo library permissions are used only to attach images to your transactions.

You can reset

A built-in reset clears all local data. Export first if you want a copy.
